## Rank-ordered top-3 live leads

1. **H5 immune/cytokine** — newest data, mechanistically testable, plausible coupling to GDF15/maternal immune axis.
2. **H3 progesterone/trigeminal decrease** — only hypothesis with objective human chemosensory ERP + psychophysical dissociation; small n but directly falsifiable.
3. **H7 cognitive/high-level** — multiple converging human findings (P3, OID deficit, GM remodeling in olfactory regions); no hormone assumption needed.

## Single cheapest decisive experiment

A single-site cross-sectional first-trimester study (wks 5–14, n≈50 pregnant vs 25 controls) drawing one blood sample for **concomitant hCG + GDF15 + progesterone/estradiol** and measuring (a) psychophysical thresholds and hedonics for **ecologically relevant spoilage/pathogen odors** vs safe controls, plus (b) NVP severity. This simultaneously attacks H1, H4, H5, H8 and supplies the missing hormone–psychophysics correlation for H2/H3. Human causal data remain absent for H1–H5, H8, H9 until intervention/mediational designs exist.

## Material update to the direct (non-subagent) audit

The direct `03_hypothesis_audit.md` ranked H4 (hCG) as the top lead. The subagent run surfaced Fejzo 2023 (*Nature* 625:760, GDF15 causal for NVP) and Petry 2018 (hCG not elevated in vomiting), which reframe hCG as likely **epiphenomenal** and make GDF15 the stronger temporal/mechanistic candidate. Treat H4 as a GDF15-testing design, not hCG-specific.
