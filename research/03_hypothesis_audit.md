## Rank-ordered live leads

1. **H4 hCG-timing** — strongest temporal match, zero direct tests, cheapest decisive design (concurrent hormone + psychophysics). This is the repo's named strongest solo angle.
2. **H7 cognitive/high-level** — moderate strength, already supported by ERP + meta-analytic cognitive overlap; testable by adding cognitive battery to olfactory tests (Albaugh 2022 explicitly recommends this).
3. **H8 Garcia-effect conditioning** — untested and mechanistically sufficient without hyperosmia (Cameron 2014 notes conditioning "does not require hyperosmia"); one prospective study could resolve the Heinrichs/Hummel contradiction.

## Single cheapest decisive experiment

Concurrent **hCG (and estradiol/progesterone) assay + psychophysical threshold/hedonic test using ecologically relevant food/spoilage odors** in first-trimester women across weeks 5–12. Fixes three gaps at once: (a) no concomitant hormone+smell study exists; (b) test odors are non-food (PEA/n-butanol) though complaints are food/spoilage; (c) within-subject timing avoids the collapsed-trimester confounding. Cost: one lab, repeated measures, n≥40 to detect the moderate effect implied by H4.
