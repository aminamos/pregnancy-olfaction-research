## Strongest for / against per claim

- **C1:** FOR Nordin 2004 (67% early). AGAINST Gilbert & Wysocki 1991 (13,610 rated own smell lower; largest N in field).
- **C2:** FOR Luvara & Murizi 1961 (47, blast-injection; confounded by nasal engorgement). AGAINST Albaugh 2022 pooled (SMD 0.19, crosses 0; I²=81%); Cameron 2014b signal-detection d′ null.
- **C3:** FOR Albaugh 2022 (SMD −0.32, p=0.05) + Muluh 2024. AGAINST Fornazieri 2019 (124/50, no diff); Gilbert & Wysocki 1991 (no general effect).
- **C4:** FOR Kölble 2001 / Ochsenbein-Kölble 2007 / Cameron 2007 (coffee, cigarette, rum, clove less pleasant). AGAINST Albaugh 2022 coffee meta (null, but k=2, I²=97.25% — uninformative, not negative).

## Resolution

1. The defensible model is amplified **central appraisal**, not a better sensor: lower pleasantness + higher disgust + liberal detection criterion, with sensory ERP components (N1/P1) unchanged (Olofsson 2005).
2. The two "contradictions" are measurement-level, not real: self-report ≠ threshold because self-report tracks hedonic salience, and ID deficit tracks cognition (semantic/verbal), not sensitivity.
3. Trimester is not a reliable moderator for ID (Q_M=0.25, p=0.88) but is for intensity (Muluh 2024 meta-regression); self-report peaks early and resolves postpartum (Nordin 2004).
